Mofu Guard

Check what you sign, before you sign it. A free browser extension from Mofu.

What it does

  • Stops risky signatures. When a site asks your wallet to approve tokens, approve a whole NFT collection, sign a permit or a blind signature, Mofu Guard shows what it really does first, in plain English. You choose: don't sign, or continue to your wallet.
  • Flags the usual drainer moves. Unlimited approvals, approvals and permits to a plain wallet instead of an app, marketplace orders that ask nothing in return, and blind eth_sign requests.
  • Checks the contract on Robinhood Chain. For requests on Robinhood Chain it adds Mofu's Token Scanner verdict: can the token mint, pause, block wallets or change its code? Elsewhere it still decodes the request.
  • Hover to scan. Hover a contract address on any page (X, a DEX, a chat) to see its scan, or select one and right-click → Scan with Mofu.

It never holds your keys, never signs anything and never sends a transaction. It only reads and warns.

Install

Mofu Guard is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until it's listed, you can install the early version by hand in Chrome, Brave or Edge:

  1. Download mofu-guard.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin Mofu Guard from the puzzle icon so you can reach its switches.

The source is open in the Mofu repository, so you can read exactly what it does before you install it.

What it sends, and to whom

  • Signing requests are decoded inside your browser. Nothing about your wallet, your balance or your history is sent anywhere.
  • For Robinhood Chain requests and hover scans, the contract or spender address being checked is sent to mofu.works, which reads that contract from public Robinhood Chain nodes. Mofu keeps a count of checks per IP address for the day, and caches a result for five minutes.
  • When you choose Don't sign on a warning, the extension adds one to a public counter on mofu.works/stats. Nothing about the request, the site or your wallet is sent with it.
  • Your two switches (protection and hover scan) are saved in your browser's extension storage.

Limits

Mofu Guard works with wallets that use the standard browser provider (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and most others). A site that talks to a wallet in an unusual way, or a signature type Mofu doesn't know, may not be caught. It's a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee: always read what your wallet shows before you confirm.
