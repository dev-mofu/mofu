Any other token using the Mofu name or mascot isn't ours. Before you buy anything, compare the address character by character: copycats often match only the first and last few.

Mofu has no Telegram group, no Discord server and no support accounts that message you first.

The only thing Mofu asks you to sign

Connecting a wallet for holder perks shows exactly this message (your address and a random nonce at the end). It isn't a transaction and costs no gas: 

mofu.works wants you to confirm you own this wallet.

Signing only proves ownership to unlock $MOFU holder perks. It is not a transaction, costs no gas and gives no one access to your funds.

If a site asks you to approve tokens, send a transaction or sign anything else "to verify" or "to claim", it isn't Mofu.

What Mofu never does

  • Never asks for your seed phrase or private key, anywhere, for any reason.
  • Never DMs you first, and never offers support or "early access" in private messages.
  • Never runs airdrops or claims that need you to connect and approve.
  • Never asks you to send tokens or ETH to receive something back.
  • Never tells you to buy or sell. Mofu's tools show what the chain says; you decide.
