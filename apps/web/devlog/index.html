Nov 12, 2026

Mofu Guard

A browser extension that checks what your wallet is about to sign. Approvals, permits, approve-for-all, marketplace orders and blind signatures stop at a plain-English panel first; unlimited approvals and plain-wallet spenders are flagged; Robinhood Chain tokens get their scan. Hover any contract address for its scan.

Nov 12, 2026

Holder roadmap vote

$MOFU holders vote on what Mofu builds next. Each vote counts with the wallet's balance when results are tallied, so moving tokens can't vote twice. Results are public.

Nov 12, 2026

Token Scanner full report and transaction explainer

Scans now show the top 10 holders (pools and burns labeled), a concentration check and who created the token, as a holder perk; everyone gets the summary. Paste a transaction hash to see transfers, mints, burns and approvals decoded, with risky approvals flagged.
