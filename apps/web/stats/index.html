How these are counted

  • Each counter goes up by one when the thing happens on mofu.works or through the MCP server. Counts only: no addresses, no content.
  • "Risky signatures stopped" counts the times someone chose Don't sign on a Mofu Guard warning.
  • On-chain facts come from Robinhood Chain (holders through GoldRush). The page refreshes them every few minutes.
